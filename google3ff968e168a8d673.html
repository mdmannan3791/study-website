Main subjects

PHYSICS

First, try to understand the main idea of each topic. Do not only memorize the formulas. Understanding makes Physics much easier.

Write the important formulas in one place. Learn what each symbol means and when to use the formula. Revise them regularly.

Solve different types of Physics problems every day. Use simple diagrams when studying Physics. Diagrams help us understand many topics more clearly.

Revise old topics along with new ones. Try to solve questions without looking at the answer. This helps you remember the lessons for a longer time.

Chemistry

First, understand the basic ideas of each chapter. Try to know why a reaction or process happens. This makes Chemistry easier to learn.

Write important chemical reactions in a separate place. Practice writing them regularly.

Chemistry has many mathematical problems. Practice formulas and calculations step by step.

Write important points, formulas, reactions, and rules in short notes.Revise old topics after learning new ones.

Try to recall reactions and formulas without looking at your notes. Regular revision helps you remember them longer.

Higher Mathematics

Understand the formula before using it. Learn where and why it is used.Solve a few problems every day. Regular practice makes Math easier.

Solve problems step by step. Do not skip important steps.

Check your wrong answers and find your mistakes. This helps you improve.
